### 👨‍💻 About Me

After a decade as an independent **Certified Public Accountant (CPA)**, advising SMEs on tax strategy and financial planning, I made a deliberate pivot into **Data Science and Cloud Computing** — not as a career reset, but as the natural evolution of a decade spent turning numbers into decisions.

I completed a **Postgraduate Diploma in Data Science & AI** (UNSAM) and have since built hands-on experience across the full ML lifecycle: from preprocessing and modeling to deploying production-ready pipelines with **AWS SageMaker**, **Generative AI (RAG/LLMs)**, and serverless architectures.

Over the last year I specialized in **cloud computing**, starting with several **Google Cloud** certifications and moving most recently into **AWS**, where I completed **AWS re/Start**, earned my **AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner** and **AWS Certified AI Practitioner** credentials, and became an **AWS Academy Accredited Instructor** after obtaining the **AWS Academy Certified Educator** certification. I currently teach AWS Cloud Architecture and AI programs at Campus Norte, UNC.

My CPA background remains an asset, not a footnote: it's what lets me connect a model's technical output to a business KPI a finance team actually cares about.
